What Makes the Spirit of Adventure Yacht Trip Different From a Marina Cruise?

A practical comparison of desert adventure, coastal sightseeing and evening entertainment in Dubai.

TravelEase | Dubai Travel Guide | 2026

Dubai offers different ways to enjoy the outdoors, from sailing along the waterfront to exploring the Arabian desert. A Marina cruise focuses on coastal views, time on the water and the city's impressive skyline. In contrast, a desert safari combines off-road excitement, traditional activities, live entertainment and a barbecue dinner. Each option offers a different way to spend time in Dubai.

For travellers comparing these options, the main difference is the type of outing they prefer. The desert itinerary offered by Dubai City Sightseeing features Spirit of Adventure, with a two-seater dune buggy ride, a camel ride, refreshments, cultural performances and dinner. This makes it a suitable option for visitors who want several activities in one outing rather than a relaxing cruise.

At a Glance

Spirit of Adventure: Desert driving, camel riding, cultural shows and barbecue dinner.

Marina cruise: Waterfront views, skyline photography and time on the water.

Choose based on your preferred setting, activity level and available time.

What Makes This Desert Safari Different From a Marina Cruise?

The biggest difference is the setting and the activities. A Marina cruise takes place on the water, with views of Dubai Marina's towers, waterfront residences and surrounding coastline. The desert safari takes visitors to Al Margham, where the focus is on driving across sandy terrain, enjoying traditional entertainment and experiencing the desert atmosphere.

Here are the main highlights:

  • Off-road adventure: Enjoy a two-seater dune buggy ride after an introductory briefing.
  • Traditional activity: Experience a camel ride after the buggy adventure.
  • Cultural entertainment: Watch Tanoura dance, belly dance and a fire show.
  • Desert dining: Enjoy a barbecue dinner with vegetarian and non-vegetarian options.
  • Arabian refreshments: Welcome refreshments include Arabic coffee, tea and dates.

A Marina cruise may suit travellers who prefer a slower pace and coastal photography. The desert outing is more suitable for those looking for a combination of outdoor and evening entertainment.

How Long Does the Tour Take, and When Does It Start?

Dubai City Sightseeing gives a six-hour duration and a 2 PM start time, with daily departures and a starting price of AED 2,800. However, another section of the tour mentions an overall duration of eight hours. Confirm the current schedule, pickup arrangements and final price before making plans.

The company also mentions hotel or accommodation pickup and transfer facilities. Check whether transportation is included in your chosen option and confirm the expected return time before departure.

Who Should Choose a Desert Safari Instead of a Marina Cruise?

This desert outing may suit:

  • Travellers who enjoy driving and outdoor adventures.
  • Couples looking for an evening with dinner and entertainment.
  • Friends who want several activities during one trip.
  • Visitors interested in camel riding and traditional performances.
  • Guests who want to combine desert scenery with a barbecue meal.

A Marina cruise may be a better fit for visitors who prefer spending time on the water, taking waterfront photographs or enjoying a relaxed sightseeing activity. The right choice depends on the atmosphere and pace you prefer.

What Activities Can You Enjoy During the Desert Safari?

1. Spirit of Adventure Starts With a Dune Buggy Ride

The dune buggy ride is one of the main attractions of this desert safari. After reaching the desert, participants receive an instructor's briefing about operating the buggy. The tour specifies a two-seater vehicle, allowing visitors to explore the sandy landscape and take photographs against the golden dunes.

Travellers researching Dune Buggy Dubai activities should distinguish this combined package from a standalone buggy rental. This outing brings the driving activity together with a camel ride, camp entertainment and dinner, offering variety beyond off-road driving alone.

2. Experience Camel Riding and Desert Scenery

Following the buggy ride, guests can enjoy a camel ride. The slower pace provides a contrast to driving across the dunes and offers a chance to appreciate a traditional form of desert transport.

The surrounding landscape also creates opportunities for photographs. Visitors should follow the tour team's instructions and allow enough time for each activity instead of rushing between stops.

3. Enjoy Live Shows and a BBQ Dinner

The evening continues at the desert camp, where the advertised entertainment includes Tanoura dance, belly dance and a fire show. Henna application is also mentioned for women.

Dinner is another key part of the itinerary. The package confirms a barbecue dinner with vegetarian and non-vegetarian choices. This combination of outdoor activities, cultural performances and dining distinguishes the safari from a typical sightseeing cruise.

Travel Tips Before Your Desert Adventure

A little preparation can make the outing more comfortable. Keep these practical points in mind:

  • Wear comfortable clothing: Breathable cotton clothing is recommended. Avoid loose accessories or garments that could interfere with activities.
  • Check driving eligibility: The tour FAQ states a minimum age of 16 for driving the dune buggy and five for non-driving passengers. Confirm the current requirements before travelling.
  • Consider physical comfort: The tour advises people with back problems not to participate.
  • Confirm the schedule: The stated start time is 2 PM, but verify pickup and the expected return time.
  • Review the cancellation policy: The published terms state that cancellations made at least 72 hours before departure incur no charge, while cancellations within 72 hours incur 100% charges. Confirm the terms applicable to your reservation.
  • Check the price: The starting price is AED 2,800. Verify the current total cost and your selected arrangements before confirming.
